# Case 1 — general answer (written before the plan analysis was run)

Same host, same evidence as the plan case (`lit-*` ids from the product-path reads recorded in
`case1_draft.json`). No `plan_analysis` was consulted while writing this. It is kept verbatim as
the comparison point.

**Goal.** An ECM-based material bridges and temporarily supports a defect, lets cells in, and
hands function over to newly formed ECM; scar minimisation is long-term; in vitro now. Material,
cell type and format are not chosen.

**Plan.**

1. *Ingress.* Place the scaffold against a cell source so cells must enter; measure nuclei by
   depth. Crosslinking and cell source are the main variables (fetal > eschar > adult fibroblasts
   in deposition and infiltration, lit-34e29aabd827).
2. *New vs original ECM.* Pulse-chase label newly synthesised protein and read collagen
   specifically. The Dupuytren Discussion says processed new collagen sat in the constructs while
   the medium held mainly proforms after an overnight label and 3-hour chase (lit-670133a47aa6,
   lit-eba9427a4c2d), so **measure both construct and medium**. mRNA ratios do not track the new
   chain ratio (lit-cd732cb414f8, lit-3011dae7c880), so gene expression is not the readout.
3. *Degradation.* Cells × broad MMP inhibitor × crosslinking, with CHP staining (denatured
   collagen, proteolysis or mechanical, lit-42a676c7e0c2) and uptake of fragments (MRC2 route,
   lit-a0879275b79e).
4. *Bridge / handover.* Gap-spanning construct: continuity over time with and without cells,
   read with remaining original material and new collagen.
5. *Contraction/fibrosis.* TGF-β1 challenge then withdrawal; α-SMA and contraction; serum as a
   condition (serum TGF-β, lit-3011dae7c880, lit-670133a47aa6).

**Which first.** Experiment 2 (new vs original ECM), because without separating new from original
material neither degradation nor handover can be measured.

**Hypotheses** considered: cell proteolysis, cell-independent loss, contraction masking mass
change, crosslinking trade-off, myofibroblast state coupling contraction and reduced degradation,
handover before continuity loss. They can co-occur.
