# DRAFT (pre-review)

## Goal
Distinguish why the myofibroblast phenotype (alpha-SMA stress fibres, contractility) persists after TGF-b1 washout in primary human fibroblasts: residual TGF-b1, cell-to-cell signalling, persistent cell-state change.

## Explanations
- E1 Residual TGF-b1 (adsorbed to plastic/ECM). No pack source; general knowledge.
- E2 Autocrine/paracrine TGF-b loop (S6, S7).
- E3 Persistent intrinsic state, chromatin (S3, S4).
- E4 Mechanical maintenance by stiff plastic (S2, S8).
- E5 Senescence (S5).
All can coexist.

## Experiments
1. Post-washout TbRI inhibitor (SB-431542 10 uM) and pan-TGF-b antibody. E1/E2 predict reversal; E3/E4/E5 predict persistence (S1: SD-208 did not reverse TGF-b1-induced state).
2. Conditioned medium transfer to naive cells. E2 predicts naive activation.
3. Replate on soft substrate. E4 predicts loss; E3 predicts persistence (S4).
4. Senescence markers + navitoclax (S5).

## Controls
Untreated, continuous TGF-b1, vehicle, isotype IgG.

## Decisions
If inhibitor reverses -> ligand-dependent; then test source with CM. If not -> intrinsic or mechanical; replate soft.

## Priority
Experiment 1, because it splits ligand-dependent from ligand-independent.
